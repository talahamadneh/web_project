📍 Popular Areas

Sultanahmet

Visit Hagia Sophia (25€), Blue Mosque (Free), and Topkapi Palace (20€).
Estimated Entry Fee: Free – 25€

Taksim Square

Free to enter. Restaurants & cafés range from 10€ – 20€ per meal.
Estimated Cost: 10€ – 20€

Balat

Free to explore on foot. Local cafés range from 5€ – 10€.
Estimated Cost: 5€ – 10€

🍴 Famous Restaurants

  • Sultanahmet Köftecisi – Traditional Turkish meatballs. Rating: 4.5/5
    Avg Price: $8–15 per person
  • 360 Istanbul – Panoramic rooftop dining. Rating: 4.7/5
    Avg Price: $30–60 per person

🛍️ Shopping Spots

  • Grand Bazaar – Haggle for souvenirs, spices, and textiles.
    Estimated Budget: 5€ – 50€
  • Istinye Park – Designer shopping & luxury brands.
    Typical Spend: 50€ – 300€+

🚢 Activities to Try

  • Bosphorus Cruise – Scenic 1 to 2 hour boat ride.
    Price: 10€ – 25€
  • Turkish Hammam – Traditional bath and massage experience.
    Price: 20€ – 40€
